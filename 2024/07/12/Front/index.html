About Me

Hi, I am an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. I am also a maintainer of RL-Kernel , an open-source library of bitwise-consistent CUDA/Triton kernels for RL post-training. Previously, I was an Research Assistant in the Reinforcement Learning and Bandits Lab at NYCU and AI Software Engineer at Rayleigh Vision Intelligence, where I built multimodal wafer-inspection systems. My research interests include Reinforcement Learning, Large Reasoning Models and Machine Learning Systems. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from NYCU, advised by Prof. Ping-Chun Hsieh, and was an exchange student at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in fall 2024.
